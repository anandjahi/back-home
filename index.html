Our Why

Our dollars can do more.

Every year, people in the US send over $100 billion to loved ones abroad. These remittances are a critical lifeline, but they have limits.

Back Home is building infrastructure that allows money sent back home to be:

Invested in lasting solutions

Not spent on everyday expenses

Returned with interest

Building wealth for diasporas at the same time

Pooled for economies of scale

So no family has to go it alone

We’re still figuring out the model, but this is our best guess:

Back Home will create place-based funds for diasporas and their allies to invest in. We’ll collect and pool investments from individual and institutional investors in the United States. Then, we’ll lend that money to intermediary organizations in the corresponding country, who will lend the funds to end borrowers. Based on the terms of the loan, the end borrowers will repay the intermediaries, the intermediaries will repay Back Home, and Back Home will repay its investors.

Yes. Calvert Impact and Working Capital for Community Needs raise investment capital from individuals and institutional investors in the US, pool the funds, and lend to values-driven borrowers abroad.

With over 20 years of experience in the social impact and non-profit sectors, Anand Jahi (né Goodridge) has spent his career supporting working and middle class families build wealth by leveraging strategies that benefit the environment and broader community. As an independent consultant, Anand has advised philanthropic foundations, mission-driven businesses, community loan funds, and national non-profit organizations.

The descendant of Barbadian immigrants to the United States, Anand is passionate about building bridges between the island and the diaspora that are rooted in mutual benefit, respect, and justice.
